# MoonJab Pricing

> MoonJab is an AI-powered career platform for university students and young professionals in Latin America.
> Website: https://moonjab.com | Contact: hey@moonjab.com

## Free Plan (Guest Mode)

- Price: $0/month
- No credit card required
- Features:
  - CV builder with 1 template (Creativo)
  - 3 interview simulations per month
  - Basic vocational diagnosis (RIASEC)
  - Guest mode access (no account required)

## Pro Plan (Premium)

- Price: $5 USD/month
- Billed monthly, cancel anytime
- No long-term commitment
- Features:
  - Unlimited CV builder with ALL professional templates (Harvard, Modern, Minimal, Professional, Creativo)
  - Unlimited AI interview simulations
  - Detailed AI feedback on every interview response
  - Complete RIASEC vocational diagnosis
  - Personalized AI career coach (24/7)
  - Unlimited PDF export (high quality, ATS-optimized)
  - Priority customer support

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from Settings > Subscription at any time. No penalties, no minimum commitment.

**What payment methods are accepted?**
Visa, Mastercard, and major credit/debit cards via Stripe. Payments are processed securely.

**Is there a student discount?**
Yes, 50% off for students with a verified .edu email. Contact hey@moonjab.com with your institutional email to get the discount code.

**Which countries is MoonJab available in?**
Available across Latin America: Peru, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Ecuador, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay, Venezuela.
